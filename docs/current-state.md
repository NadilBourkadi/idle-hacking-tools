## Bottleneck model

1. **Primary: accumulated HP attrition across long streaks** — losses consistently start well below full HP (starting HP 17–65%, median ~30% across the 24–27 July deaths). Moved twice: the Phoenix Shell (23 July) lifted the ceiling ~101 → ~110 and attrition onset 60–76 → 78–90; player level then carried it to a settled 105–110. Residual form is deep-fight net drain where `prg` decay outpaces gear Regen. The 27 July package attacked this from three sides at once — hardware Regen +14.9%, Defense +6.1%, and Barrier from **0 to 280** — and **read out at +20.5 death streaks with net drain per round now negative (−2.8)**. Regeneration is therefore the confirmed win condition for this build, which is why candidates that spend Regen or Max HP for Defense are held even at a positive ceiling delta. **Settled ceiling as of 6 August: mean death streak 189.2 over 47 post-Driver deaths** (`driver-ab-2026-08-03` close) — quote this, not the retired 173.5/167.6/155.0/140.2 figures. The window is a bundle (Driver craft + 5 Aug 290K-chip package + VLAN Def + Snapshot Rollback, 41 procs); the sim decomposition attributes ~+1.9 of the +12.6 to the craft itself, the rest to hardware/procs.
2. **Secondary: hit reliability — RESOLVED 29 July, priced.** The saturation reading was an artefact of a biased denominator (`pm` flags only first-attack misses). The unbiased fit on 2-attack rounds gives `logit(hit) = −0.164 + 1.420·ln(Acc/Eva)`; true per-attack hit rate at the streak-120–159 faces is 65–68% — not saturated. `CRAFT_WEIGHTS_PCT["Acc"]` is **measured at 0.21** (`assumptions` register records the full 0.14 → 0.19 → 0.115 → 0.21 path).
3. **Tertiary: incoming burst** against the thin Def/Barrier flank (one 76%-start-HP death vs a Trojan Wall in the Shell A/B). Barrier 0 → 280 is the first direct answer to this since the Shell traded it away.
4. **Output is the deliberate cost.** Crit chance fell 51.0% → 31.5% across the day (Analyzer craft plus Vulnerability Scanner cut to 17). Roughly −10% damage per landed hit, accepted because the 22 July law holds that tempo does not move the death ceiling while recovery and mitigation do. If depth does not improve, `damage_taken` **per round** is the diagnostic — a rise there would mean attack speed is a mitigation stat and the model needs revising.

**Amendment, 28 July: regeneration has visible diminishing returns and they are depth-dependent.** The Firewall's +29.7% *listed* Regen realized +0.4% / +4.7% / +11.5% of `prg`/round at streak bands 60–85 / 86–105 / 106–130 — rising monotonically with depletion (overheal capping, `open-questions.md`). Net drain per round halved at depth (+1,723 → +887 at 106–130) but is **still positive**, so this predicts a higher ceiling, not an unbounded one. Consequence: "more regen" is no longer automatically the answer — the next large regen buy will realize less again, which is why the standing chip advice caps ECC instead of spending the whole budget.

Next levers are recovery *systems*, not more gear regen: **Snapshot Rollback** (homelab 12 — recover 25% HP after lethal damage, once per 10 fights) and **Power Conditioner** (homelab 13 — heal 1% of damage dealt per level, cap 10%, ≈1,500 HP/fight at current output). Both are now affordable: hackcoin sells at ~8.33B credits, so the whole roadmap including the **Hacking Simulator** (3.60 hc) and **Software Profiler** (1.12 hc) is ~10.6 hc of 28.
